Welcome

A warm, thoughtful space to slow down, understand what you are carrying and make room for a way forward that feels more like your own.

Sometimes life can feel too much, too uncertain, or strangely disconnected. You may know exactly what has brought you here, or simply have a feeling that something needs to change.

I offer warm, collaborative counselling for adults who would like space to understand themselves more deeply, work through difficult experiences and develop a kinder relationship with themselves.

You might be looking for support with:

  • Anxiety and overthinking
  • Low self-esteem and self-doubt
  • Relationships and boundaries
  • Life transitions
  • Stress and burnout
  • Loss or uncertainty
  • Identity and belonging
  • Feeling stuck or disconnected

You do not need to arrive with the right words. We can begin with where you are.

01 · About

About me

Counselling can offer something rare: time that is entirely yours, without pressure to perform, reassure anyone else or have an immediate answer.

My way of working is relational, thoughtful and down to earth. We pay attention to your own experience — what has shaped you, what is happening now and what you may need next.

Some sessions may feel reflective and quiet; others may be practical, emotional or energising. The work can move at your pace, with curiosity rather than judgement.

A quiet place to talk, reflect and be yourself.

02 · Practice

How we might work

There is no single correct way to do therapy. We begin by understanding what has brought you here and what you would like to be different.

Sessions create space to notice patterns, explore feelings that can be hard to express elsewhere and make connections between past experiences and present-day responses.
