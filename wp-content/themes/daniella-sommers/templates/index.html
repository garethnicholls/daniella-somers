01 · About

About me

I believe counselling can offer something rare: time that is entirely yours, without pressure to perform, reassure anyone else or have an immediate answer.

My way of working is relational, thoughtful and down to earth. Rather than trying to fit you into a fixed formula, we pay attention to your own experience — what has shaped you, what is happening now and what you may need next.

Some sessions may feel reflective and quiet; others may be practical, emotional or energising. The work can move at your pace, with curiosity rather than judgement.

Most importantly, I want the relationship between us to feel human. Therapy can involve difficult conversations, but it can also include relief, humour, clarity and the experience of being genuinely understood.

02 · Practice

How we might work

There is no single correct way to do therapy. We begin by understanding what has brought you here and what you would like to be different.

Our sessions offer space to notice patterns, explore feelings that can be hard to express elsewhere and make connections between past experiences and present-day responses.

I work with adults on both a short-term and open-ended basis. We can review the work together as we go, so therapy remains purposeful without becoming rigid.
