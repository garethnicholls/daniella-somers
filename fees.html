Fees

For this design concept, a 50-minute individual counselling session is shown as £60.

Before booking a first full session, the demo includes a complimentary 15-minute introductory call. This is a simple opportunity to briefly talk about what you are looking for, ask questions and get a sense of whether working together feels right.

Sessions would normally take place at the same time each week, with flexibility discussed where needed. A small number of reduced-fee spaces could also be mentioned here if that reflects Daniella’s actual practice.

The £60 fee, reduced-fee wording, cancellation terms and availability are placeholder content for design review only and must be confirmed before launch.
