About me

I believe therapy works best when you can bring your whole self into the room and feel met with curiosity rather than judgement.

This demo imagines Daniella as a warm, relational counsellor working with adults who may be dealing with anxiety, relationship difficulties, burnout, identity questions, loss, low confidence or a general sense of feeling stuck.

My approach is collaborative and grounded. I am interested in how your past and present connect, the patterns that may once have protected you, and the parts of your experience that have not always had enough space or language.

Some sessions may feel reflective and quiet; others may be more practical or emotional. There is no pressure to move faster than feels right. The aim is not to turn you into a different person, but to help you understand yourself with greater clarity and kindness.

Most importantly, I want the therapeutic relationship to feel human. Therapy can include difficult conversations, but it can also bring relief, humour, new perspectives and the experience of being genuinely understood.
