My practice

There is no single correct way to do therapy. We begin by understanding what has brought you here, what life feels like now and what you would like to be different.

Our sessions can offer space to notice repeating patterns, explore feelings that are difficult to express elsewhere and make connections between earlier experiences and present-day responses.

I work in a relational and integrative way. That means the work is shaped around you rather than asking you to fit a fixed method. We may spend time reflecting on relationships and identity, noticing how anxiety shows up in the body, making sense of difficult experiences or thinking together about boundaries and practical change.

Therapy is collaborative. I will not sit silently while you try to work everything out alone, and I will not tell you how to live. Instead, we create a thoughtful space in which new understanding and choices can become possible.
