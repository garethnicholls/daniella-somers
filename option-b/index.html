Therapy changed my own life. That experience of trust, empathy and safety is at the heart of the way I practise today.

01 / My journey

From client to counsellor.

My journey into counselling began in the client’s chair. Seeking therapy myself was a life-changing experience, and the secure relationship I built with my therapist gave me the confidence to begin studying counselling at university in 2019.

During my studies I volunteered with Getaway Girls, later working as a Complex Needs Support Worker. I have also supported students in a professional university counselling role alongside private practice.

02 / How I practice

Safety, insight and change.

Therapeutic relationship

I place trust and empathy at the centre, creating a nurturing space where you can feel seen, heard and listened to.

Psychodynamic therapy

We can explore the origins of patterns and behaviours, looking at the deeper “why” behind what is happening now.

CBT strategies

Practical tools can help challenge negative thoughts, manage anxiety or depression and support change in the here and now.
