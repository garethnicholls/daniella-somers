My own journey through therapy inspired me to become a counsellor — and still shapes the safe, supportive relationship I aim to offer clients today.

01 / My journey

It started in the client’s chair.

Seeking therapy myself was a life-changing experience. The relationship I built with my therapist was rooted in trust, empathy and the autonomy to take responsibility for my own future. Feeling secure within that relationship gave me the confidence to begin studying counselling at university in 2019.

While studying, I volunteered with Getaway Girls, which developed into a role as a Complex Needs Support Worker. Alongside private practice, I have also worked in a professional university counselling role supporting students with their mental health and wellbeing.

Those beginnings remain central to my practice today: a safe, non-judgmental relationship where you can feel heard, explore your thoughts and work towards meeting yourself with greater kindness and self-compassion.

02 / How I practice

A relationship that supports reflection, insight and change.

1

Safety first

I place trust and empathy at the centre, creating a nurturing space where you feel seen, heard and listened to.

2

Explore the deeper why

Through psychodynamic therapy, we can look at the origins of patterns, habits and behaviours formed earlier in life and how they affect you now.

3

Support change in the present

Using CBT strategies, I can help you challenge negative thoughts, manage anxiety or depression and build practical ways of responding differently.
